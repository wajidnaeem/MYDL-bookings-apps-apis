Protection rules

Clear rules, agreed before anyone pays.

Each provider sets their own terms once. They apply to every new visit and the client sees them before checkout.

Late arrival

The provider chooses how many minutes after the start time a client counts as late, and the late fee that applies.

The fee can be charged to the card used for the deposit.

No-show

If the client does not attend, the provider can file a no-show claim and keep the deposit under the accepted policy.

Clients can report a provider who did not attend.

Cancellation

Cancel at least 24 hours before the visit and the deposit is refunded. Inside 24 hours, the provider keeps it.

If the provider cancels, the client is always refunded.

Disputes

Either side can raise a dispute and attach evidence. An administrator reviews the visit, payments and attendance, then decides.

You get an alert when the decision is made.

Questions

Good to know.

Where is my deposit while I wait?

Your card payment is processed by Stripe and the deposit is held until the visit is completed, cancelled or decided in a dispute. You can see its status in Payments at any time.

What if the QR code doesn't work?

Each code expires 10 minutes after the provider creates it. Ask the provider to refresh it, or type the code into the app instead of scanning.

Can I get my deposit back if I cancel?

Yes, if you cancel at least 24 hours before the visit. Inside 24 hours the provider keeps the deposit. The app tells you which applies before you confirm.

What if the provider doesn't show up?

Report it from the visit in the client app. It opens a dispute, and an administrator reviews it and decides on a refund.

Do I need an account?

Yes. Providers invite clients by the email on their MYDL account, so create your account and verify your email first.
